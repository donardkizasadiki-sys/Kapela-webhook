Privacy Policy for Kapela Wilondja Messenger Bot

Last updated: May 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Kapela Bot collects only the information necessary to respond to your messages:

  • Facebook User ID and Name
  • Messages you send to our Facebook Page
  • Time and date of messages

2. How We Use Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to your messages automatically
  • Provide customer service via Messenger
  • Improve bot responses

3. Data Storage

We do not store message content permanently. Messages are processed in real-time and logs are deleted after 30 days.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is only shared with Meta to deliver Messenger services.

5. User Rights

You can request deletion of your data by messaging our Page "Delete my data".

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: kapelawilondja@gmail.com
